Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Le Zap Publisher ("the app") is a personal publishing tool. It is used only by its owner, Léopold Becuwe, to upload the owner's own short videos to the owner's own accounts: the TikTok accounts @lezapdevicecity and @thewantedtape, and the YouTube channels "Le Zap de Vice City" and "The Wanted Tape". It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

TikTok

Data we access

When the owner logs in with TikTok, the app receives the access token and basic profile information (display name, avatar, open ID) of the owner's own account, through the scopes user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish.

How we use it

Only to upload and publish the owner's videos to the owner's account. The token is stored on the owner's own computer only. It is never sold, shared or sent to third parties.

Deletion

The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions). Stored tokens are then deleted.

YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services to upload the owner's videos as YouTube Shorts to the owner's own YouTube channels. By using the app, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Data we access

When the owner signs in with Google (OAuth 2.0), the app receives an access token for the owner's own YouTube channel, through the scopes youtube.upload, youtube.readonly and youtube. With them, the app reads the channel's name and ID, uploads videos, sets their title, description and thumbnail, and can delete a video the owner uploaded with the app.

How we use and store it

The data is used only to publish the owner's videos to the owner's channel. Tokens and upload receipts (video ID, title, date) are stored only on the owner's own computer. The app does not sell, share or send YouTube data to any third party, does not use cookies or tracking, and does not collect any data about YouTube viewers or other users.

Deletion and revocation

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time on the Google security settings page. Stored tokens are then deleted from the owner's computer within 7 days, and receipts can be deleted on request.
