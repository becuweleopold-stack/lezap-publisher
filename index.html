Le Zap Publisher

Le Zap Publisher is a private tool used by the creator of the TikTok account @lezapdevicecity to publish his own short videos (GTA 6 gaming highlights) to his own TikTok account, at scheduled times.

It also publishes the same videos as Shorts to the creator's own YouTube channels, through YouTube API Services.

It is used only by the account owner. It does not collect data from other users.
